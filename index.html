Supporting the East Whittier Middle School band community

Why Mrs. Carnahan Matters

Mrs. Carnahan has been an important part of the East Whittier Middle School band community for many years. Students and families who value the program can use this page to share their support and explain what the band program means to them.

Our request: Please consider the experiences of students, families, and alumni before making any decision about the future of the EWMS band program and Mrs. Carnahan's role in it.

A lasting impact

Supporters describe Mrs. Carnahan as a dedicated band teacher who has helped build a strong music program and encouraged students to participate in band. If you have personally experienced her teaching, consider sharing a specific memory or example of how the program affected you.

Why the band program is valuable

  • It gives students an opportunity to learn and perform music.
  • It creates a community where students can work together toward a common goal.
  • It can encourage students to continue music beyond middle school.
  • It gives students a place to develop confidence, responsibility, and teamwork.

About the history: Supporters state that Mrs. Carnahan has worked with the EWMS band program for more than 40 years. This website presents that as a community claim unless independently documented by the school or district.
