A Lasting Impact

When Mrs. Carnahan began working with the EWMS music program, it was a much smaller program with relatively few students. Over the years, through her dedication, teaching, and commitment to her students, she helped the program grow into the thriving music program it is today.

For more than 40 years, Mrs. Carnahan has been an important part of the EWMS music community. During that time, she has helped introduce countless students to music, encouraged students to participate, and created opportunities for them to learn, perform, and grow.

Her work has helped make the EWMS music program a meaningful part of the school community. Many students have been drawn to the program because of the positive environment she has helped create and the enthusiasm she brings to music education.

Why the Music Program Matters

The EWMS music program gives students opportunities to:

  • Learn and perform music.
  • Develop their musical skills and confidence.
  • Work together as an ensemble.
  • Build friendships and a sense of community.
  • Continue their interest in music throughout middle school and beyond.

Mrs. Carnahan's many years of dedication have played an important role in helping the EWMS music program reach the level it is at today.
